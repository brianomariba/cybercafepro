⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Activity Guide

Use these keyboard shortcuts anywhere on the desktop to quickly record activities. No need to open this portal!

💡 How It Works

1 Your admin has configured trackable services (like KRA, Printing, Photocopies) with keyboard shortcuts.
2 Press a keyboard shortcut anywhere on the desktop (even outside this window) and a recording popup will appear.
3 Fill in the quantity, customer name (optional), and notes, then click Record Activity.
4 At the end of the day, go to ⚡ Activity Tracker tab and click 📤 Submit & Close Day to send your records to the backend.
5 You can also click on a service card in the Activity Tracker tab to record without using a shortcut.

🚀 Pro Tips

💡 When the popup appears, the quantity field is auto-focused so you can type a number immediately.
⏎ Press Enter in the notes field to quickly submit the record.
⏰ The popup will auto-close after 60 seconds if you don't interact with it.
🔄 If the admin adds new services or changes shortcuts, they will be automatically updated on your agent.
💾 Your daily activity log is saved locally even if the app restarts. Just remember to submit before the day ends.
